## What to Watch Out For When You're Comparing Services

Not every **LinkedIn ghostwriting Australia** provider is built the same.

Watch out for:

**Overpromising on reach.** If someone guarantees you X followers in Y days, that's a red flag. Legitimate services focus on quality content and sustainable **LinkedIn growth Australia**, not vanity metrics.

**No real onboarding.** If they can write your first post before asking you a single question, they're not writing *your* content. They're writing generic content with your name on it.

**Locked in contracts with no flexibility.** Good services are confident enough in their work that they don't need to trap you.

**Writers who don't understand your industry.** A post about mortgage broking written by someone who's never heard of a serviceability buffer is going to read exactly like that.

### Questions Worth Asking Before You Sign Up

A few that save a lot of headaches later:

- Who actually writes the content?
- What's the revision process?
- How do you capture my voice?
- What happens if I hate a post?
- Can I see samples from people in my industry?

Simple questions. But the answers tell you a lot.
