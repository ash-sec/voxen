## How to Choose the Right LinkedIn Ghostwriting Service in Australia

Not all of them are created equal, and it's worth being a bit choosy here.

Ask to see examples. Not just posts they're proud of, but posts from different industries and different voices. A good ghostwriter should be able to write a stoic accountant and an enthusiastic property developer in a way that sounds nothing like each other.

Ask how they learn your voice. If the answer is vague, that's a flag. There should be some kind of onboarding process, questions, a conversation, something that gives them real material to work from.

Check if they understand LinkedIn specifically. General copywriters are fine, but LinkedIn has its own rhythm, its own formatting quirks, its own reader behaviour. Someone who also does brochures and email newsletters isn't necessarily bad, but LinkedIn content strategy in Australia rewards specialists.

And check what's included. Some LinkedIn content agencies in Australia offer strategy, some just offer writing, some offer both plus engagement support. Know what you're actually getting before you sign anything.
