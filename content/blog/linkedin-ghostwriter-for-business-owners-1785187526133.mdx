It's not just writing posts. Good LinkedIn ghostwriting involves understanding how you think, what you care about, what your clients need to hear, and then translating all of that into content that sounds like you on your best day.

## What to Look For in a LinkedIn Ghostwriter for Business Owners

Not all ghostwriters are the same. Some are generalist content writers who've pivoted to LinkedIn. Others have deep experience with the platform, understand what formats perform, and know how to write in a way that actually gets read.

A few things worth asking before you hire anyone:

**Do they interview you or just write from a brief?**
The interview matters. If they're not asking about your experiences, your opinions, your wins and failures, the content will sound generic.

**Can they show you examples of LinkedIn content they've written?**
Ideally for business owners, not just influencers or coaches. LinkedIn for business owners in Australia has specific nuances, especially if you're in professional services.

**Do they understand your industry?**
This is big if you're in something niche. LinkedIn for financial advisers, LinkedIn for mortgage brokers, LinkedIn for real estate agents, these all require a different tone and different compliance considerations to what you'd write for, say, a creative agency.

**How do they handle your voice?**
Voice matching is a skill. Ask if you can see a sample before committing to anything.
