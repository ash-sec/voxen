## How to Actually Outsource LinkedIn Posts Without It Going Wrong

A few things worth knowing before you hand over the keys.

Be available for onboarding. The first two to three weeks matter a lot. Your ghostwriter needs to understand your voice, your opinions, your hot takes. Make time for that initial conversation.

Don't over-edit everything. If you hired someone for their writing skills and then rewrite every post from scratch, you've defeated the purpose. Trust the process.

Give feedback early. If something sounds off, say so. Specific feedback like "I'd never use the word leverage" or "I always swear a bit, it's fine" helps the writer calibrate quickly.

Think about goals. Are you trying to attract clients? Recruit? Build speaker credibility? Different goals lead to different content strategies.
