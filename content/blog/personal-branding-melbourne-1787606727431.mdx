## What Strong Personal Branding on LinkedIn Actually Looks Like

Here's the thing most people get wrong. They set up a profile, maybe post once or twice, get crickets, and quietly give up.

Consistent LinkedIn content is what separates the people building genuine authority from the ones just existing on the platform.

Strong personal branding Melbourne-style means:

- **Posting regularly** (three times a week is the sweet spot most people recommend)
- **Writing in your actual voice**, not corporate jargon
- **Sharing opinions**, not just information
- **Talking about the problems your clients face**, not just what you do

It's less about broadcasting achievements and more about being genuinely useful to the people you want to attract.

### The Biggest Mistake Melbourne Professionals Make With LinkedIn

They write for their peers instead of their clients.

An accountant writing about tax legislation is writing for other accountants. But writing about what a business owner actually feels when they get an unexpected ATO notice? That lands. That builds trust. That's personal branding that actually works.
